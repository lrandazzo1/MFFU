We aim to reply within 1–2 business days.Support is handled Monday through Friday. Weekends, holidays, and busy periods may take a little longer.

Help us find the issue

When reporting a problem, please include:

  • Your league provider: ESPN, Sleeper, or Yahoo.
  • Your device model, operating system, and app version (or web browser).
  • What you were trying to do, what happened, and any error message.
  • A screenshot if it helps explain the problem.

Please do not email passwords, authentication cookies, or access tokens. Remove private information from screenshots before sending.

Before you reach out

Having trouble connecting your league?

Check that you selected the correct league provider and are following its connection instructions in the app. If an error persists, send us the message and the steps that led to it.

Questions about your data?

Read our Privacy Policy for details about how FSN handles your information. You can also email support with privacy or data deletion questions.
