Privacy Policy

Effective 8 September 2026 · Last updated 8 September 2026

Fantasy Sports Network (“FSN”, “we”) turns your existing fantasy football league into a broadcast desk. There is no FSN account, no password and no user profile. Almost everything the app knows about you lives in your own browser or on your own phone, and you can erase all of it from inside the app in one tap.

1. The short version

  • No account. You never create an FSN login. We do not ask for your name, your address or your date of birth.
  • Your League ID and provider cookies stay on your device unless you deliberately turn on League Cloud sync.
  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. The app ships none.
  • We do not sell or share personal information for advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • You can erase everything local from Setup → Privacy & Data → Erase Stored Data & Disconnect.

2. What stays on your device only

The app is a single page that runs in your browser, or inside a native shell on iOS. It keeps its working state in that device's own storage and nowhere else. That includes:

  • Your League ID and chosen provider (ESPN, Sleeper or Yahoo).
  • For private ESPN leagues, the espn_s2 and SWID cookies you paste in during setup.
  • The historical archive cache — past seasons already fetched, so the record book does not refetch them on every launch.
  • Your preferences: selected team profile, viewed season and week, notification toggles, and dismissed walkthrough state.

Every one of these is written under a key beginning fsn., fsn_ or mffu.. The Erase Stored Data & Disconnect button in Setup removes all of them from both localStorage and sessionStorage, then reloads the app clean. Nothing survives it locally.

3. What leaves your device

3.1 League data reads

To show your league, the app has to read it from your provider. Browsers cannot call ESPN, Sleeper or Yahoo directly for these endpoints, so the requests pass through relay functions on our own deployment at app.fantasysportsnetwork.app/api/*. Those requests carry your League ID and, for a private ESPN league, your ESPN cookies — sent in request headers rather than stored on the relay. The relay forwards the call, hands the response back to your device, and keeps no copy.

3.2 League Cloud sync (optional)

League Cloud lets everyone in a league share one reconstructed history instead of each member rebuilding it. When you save to it, the following is written to our database (hosted by Supabase):

StoredWhat it is
League ID and season yearThe numeric identifier from your league's URL. It is not a secret — it appears in every league link.
League historyThe reconstructed season archive: teams, managers, weekly scores, records.
Provider cookiesStored only as an AES-256-GCM encrypted envelope, never as plaintext credentials.
Share token32 random bytes minted per league. This, not the public League ID, is what authorises a read.

Writes happen only through our own server-side route, after it has verified with ESPN that the caller genuinely has access to that league. The database table has row-level security enabled with no anonymous or authenticated access policies at all: browsers never query it directly.

3.3 Push notifications (optional, off by default)

Notifications are off until you switch them on, and the permission prompt is only ever raised after you explicitly opt in. If you do turn them on, we register the device so we know where to send the alert:

  • A device identifier that is the SHA-256 hash of the push address, not the address itself.
  • The push address — an Apple APNs device token on iOS, or a Web Push subscription in a browser.
  • The League ID and, if you have picked a profile, your team ID — used only to skip alerts for someone with no rooting interest.
  • Your IANA time zone (for example America/New_York), so alerts land on your clock rather than ours.
  • Which of the three weekly windows you enabled (Tuesday, Thursday, Sunday), plus the season and week the client reported.

We also keep a small ledger of which alert was sent to which device in which week, so you are never sent the same alert twice. Turning notifications off in Setup, or revoking the permission in your operating system's settings, stops all of it.

3.4 Waitlist (marketing site only)

If you join the pre-launch waitlist on this website, we store your email address, the platform you chose, where the signup came from, and your browser's user-agent string. Commissioners may optionally pre-supply a League ID and ESPN SWID so their desk is ready on day one; those fields are blank unless you fill them in. A single welcome email is sent on first signup, and nothing else until launch.

We use a small number of service providers, each for one job:

These providers process data on our behalf to run the service. We do not sell personal information, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

5. How long we keep things

  • On your device: until you erase it, clear your browser storage, or delete the app.
  • League Cloud records: until the league is removed at a member's request.
  • Notification registrations: until you turn notifications off, or the push provider reports the address as permanently invalid, at which point it is dropped.
  • Waitlist emails: until launch, or until you ask to be removed.

6. Your choices

  • Erase everything local: Setup → Privacy & Data → Erase Stored Data & Disconnect.
  • Stop notifications: switch them off in Setup, or revoke the permission in your OS settings.
  • Never use League Cloud: it is opt-in; the app works fully without it.
  • Have cloud records or a waitlist entry deleted: email us and we will remove them.

Depending on where you live you may have additional rights over your personal information — to access it, correct it, delete it, or object to its processing. Write to us and we will honour them.

7. Children

FSN is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects, we update this page and move the “last updated” date at the top. Material changes will be surfaced in the app itself rather than only here.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request to delete something we hold: privacy@fantasysportsnetwork.app
