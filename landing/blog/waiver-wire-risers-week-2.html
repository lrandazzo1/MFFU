Waiver wire risers for week two

By FSN Desk

The first Sunday reshuffled more depth charts than usual, and the waiver order tells the story. These are low-owned names still sitting on most standard 12-team wires, not roster anchors you already start. Here are the adds worth a real bid.

The injury replacement nobody has claimed yet

Kimani Vidal stepped into first-team reps after the starter left in the third quarter, and the beat expects a committee at worst. He is rostered in a fraction of leagues right now. A backfield that just lost its lead back is exactly the kind of low-owned swing a September wire rewards, so bid before the field wakes up.

A slot target quietly trending up

Jalen McMillan ran a full route tree and drew a season high in looks with the offense playing from behind. He is still available on most wires. Emerging slot volume on a pass first team is a cheap, high floor stash, not a name you overpay for.

Stream, do not reach

  • Do not cut a starter to make room. Drop the kicker you streamed.
  • Prioritize the running back over the receiver if you can only win one claim.
  • Save a small bid for a defense against a turnover prone offense.
The wire rewards patience in September, not spending. Chase the low-owned names before the rest of your league does, and keep some budget for the injuries that have not happened yet.
