## How to read the board

The gap between the first name and the last one is the useful number here. **Terrance Ferguson** at 631,128 adds and the bottom of this list at 134,298 are not the same kind of claim, even though both are trending. The first is a bidding war and the second is a quiet add, and in a league where everyone is looking at the same public counts, the quiet add is usually where the margin is.

These counts cover every league on the platform, not yours. A player already rostered in your league is noise on this board no matter how hard the rest of the platform is chasing the name. Start from the list, then check your own wire.
