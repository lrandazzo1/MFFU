## Thursday night, before kickoff

The week 11 schedule has no Thursday night game still to come, so everything below is drawn from the 12 games that have not kicked off.

## What the wire is buying, game by game

These are Sleeper's platform-wide add counts for week 11 of the 2026 season: how many leagues across the whole platform claimed each player in the last 24 hours. Read them as demand rather than as value. Grouped by game below, in the order the week is played.

**BAL at CAR**, Sunday 1:00 PM ET.

Sleeper lists **Mark Andrews** first on the BAL depth chart at TE, which is the entire case, and 223,083 managers have already acted on it. **Justice Hill** is still listed second for BAL, so 197,775 adds are a bet on that changing against CAR rather than a reaction to it having changed.

**NO at CHI**, Sunday 1:00 PM ET.

**Cairo Santos** is the name the whole platform is already on: 437,766 adds in a day stops being a tip and starts being an auction, and NO is the game everyone paid for.

**TB at DET**, Sunday 1:00 PM ET.

First NFL season for **Detroit Lions**, and TB is the assignment that settles whether 689,805 adds were early or just loud.

**TEN at DAL**, Sunday 1:00 PM ET.

**Chimere Dike** is third at WR for TEN. At 463,365 adds the wire is pricing an injury that has not happened yet. Nobody is claiming **Elic Ayomanor** for the role he has today. He is the TEN contingency at WR, and 245,744 managers would rather own that before the news than after.

**LV at DEN**, Sunday 4:25 PM ET, divisional.

**Michael Mayer** draws DEN, a divisional game, and a defence that has seen this offence twice a year for as long as either roster has been together. Second season for **Pat Bryant**, first week the wire has cared: 248,792 adds, with LV next.
