These are Sleeper's platform-wide add counts for week 4 of the 2026 season: how many leagues across the whole platform claimed each player in the last 24 hours. Read them as demand rather than as value. Grouped by game below, in the order the week is played.

**Cleveland Browns** draws PIT, a divisional game, and a defence that has seen this offence twice a year for as long as either roster has been together.

**LAR at PHI**, Sunday 1:00 PM ET.

**Konata Mumpfield** is the name the whole platform is already on: 390,510 adds in a day stops being a tip and starts being an auction, and PHI is the game everyone paid for.
