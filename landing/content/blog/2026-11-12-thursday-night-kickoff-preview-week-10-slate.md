## What the wire is buying, game by game

These are Sleeper's platform-wide add counts for week 10 of the 2026 season: how many leagues across the whole platform claimed each player in the last 24 hours. Read them as demand rather than as value. Grouped by game below, in the order the week is played.

**NE at DET**, Sunday 9:30 AM ET.

Second season for **Corey Kiner**, first week the wire has cared: 503,136 adds, with DET next. Sleeper lists **Jake Bates** first on the DET depth chart at K, which is the entire case, and 203,872 managers have already acted on it.

**CAR at NO**, Sunday 1:00 PM ET, divisional.

**Darren Waller** draws NO, a divisional game, and a defence that has seen this offence twice a year for as long as either roster has been together.

**MIA at IND**, Sunday 1:00 PM ET.

First NFL season for **Indianapolis Colts**, and MIA is the assignment that settles whether 262,848 adds were early or just loud. Nobody is claiming **Ashton Dulin** for the role he has today. He is the IND contingency at WR, and 242,325 managers would rather own that before the news than after.

**LAR at ARI**, Sunday 4:05 PM ET, divisional.

**Konata Mumpfield** is the name the whole platform is already on: 498,015 adds in a day stops being a tip and starts being an auction, and ARI is the game everyone paid for. **Tutu Atwell** is still listed second for LAR, so 421,554 adds are a bet on that changing against ARI rather than a reaction to it having changed.

**LAC at BAL**, Monday 8:15 PM ET.

Nobody is claiming **Justice Hill** for the role he has today. He is the BAL contingency at RB, and 270,684 managers would rather own that before the news than after.
