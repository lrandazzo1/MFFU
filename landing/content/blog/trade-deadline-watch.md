The deadline is closer than the standings suggest. Records lie early. Point
differential does not. Here is who should be picking up the phone this week.

## Buy now, before the price moves

The contenders sitting on a soft bench should move first. **Christian
McCaffrey** owners in a bye week crunch will listen, and a package built around
depth beats one built around a single star. Offer two useful starters, not one
name plus filler.

## Sell the name, not the production

If your roster is a game and a half back with a bottom three point total, you
are a seller. That is not an insult, it is a plan. Call the contenders about
your veterans while the names still move rosters.

1. Lead with the player, not the pitch.
2. Ask for youth or picks, never another team's problem.
3. Take the second best offer if it comes first and clean.

## The one player who is not for sale

**Justin Jefferson** stays. A target share like his is the rarest thing in the
format, and no rebuild package returns it. Keep the star. Trade the parts around
him.
