1. What FSN is

FSN is a read-only companion for fantasy football leagues you already run on ESPN, Sleeper, or Yahoo. It reads your league's data with your permission and presents it as a broadcast-style desk: scores, matchups, standings, records, and generated written coverage.

FSN cannot change anything in your league. It sets no lineups, makes no trades, adds no players, and posts nothing to ESPN, Sleeper, or Yahoo.

2. Your league credentials

To read a private ESPN league, FSN needs the espn_s2 and SWID cookies from your own signed-in ESPN browser session. By supplying them you confirm that they are yours and that you are a member of the league you are reading. Do not supply credentials belonging to someone else.

Handling of those values is described in the Privacy Policy. In short: they are stored on your device, replayed to ESPN on your behalf, and stored on our servers only if you choose to save your league — and then only encrypted.

3. Sharing a league archive

Saving a league archive makes it retrievable by anyone who enters that numeric League ID, which is how one member unlocks the Record Book for the whole league. Do not save an archive unless you are content for it to be reachable that way. You are responsible for having your league-mates' agreement to share their team and manager names in it.
