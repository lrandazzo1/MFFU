Private league? Add ESPN cookies (optional)

Only needed if your league is private. While logged in at fantasy.espn.com, open DevTools → Application → Cookies and copy the espn_s2 and SWID values. Stored only in this browser's localStorage and sent solely to the same-origin /api/espn relay so it can read your league as you.

Current-season stories can be rewritten by gpt-4o-mini through the secure backend proxy. Historical seasons always bypass AI and use the instant deterministic desk. Successful AI stories are cached once per league, season, week and story.

● Backend AI Connected

OpenAI credentials are configured only on the server through OPENAI_API_KEY. The browser never stores or receives the secret.
