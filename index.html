WHAT IS LUCK DIFFERENTIAL?

How do I connect my league?

No tech background needed. Start with Step 1. Public leagues need only the League ID; private leagues also need Step 2. Step 3 is only for the all-time Record Book.

1

Find your League ID

Your League ID is a number ESPN gives your league. There are two easy ways to find it:

On a computer (web browser): Log in to your ESPN Fantasy league and open your league page. Look at your browser's address bar at the top — you'll see a web address containing leagueId=57155288. The number right after leagueId= is your League ID.

On the ESPN mobile app: Open your league, then tap League → League Info → League ID. The number shown there is your League ID.

Type just that number into the League ID box on the Setup screen and tap Fetch League Data.

If your league loads, you're done — it's a public league and you can skip Steps 2 and 3.
2

Private league? Get your cookies

If fetching fails with a "private league" message, ESPN needs to know it's really you. You'll copy two secret values (called espn_s2 and SWID) from your own browser.

  1. On a computer, open your ESPN Fantasy league page in Google Chrome or Safari while logged in.
  2. Press F12 (or right-click the page and choose Inspect) to open the developer panel.
  3. In that panel, open the Application tab (Chrome) or the Storage tab (Safari).
  4. On the left sidebar, expand Cookies and click https://fantasy.espn.com.
  5. In the list, find the rows named espn_s2 and SWID.
  6. Copy each long value and paste it into the matching Private League Access box on the Setup screen.
The SWID value normally includes the curly braces { } — paste it exactly as shown; we handle it either way. Your cookies stay in this browser and are only sent to ESPN through the app's own secure relay.
3

Add historical stats (.json)

Want the all-time Record Book and Rivalry Vault? Save a one-time history file from ESPN and drop it in.

  1. On a computer, open your league's history web address in a new browser tab. Tip: enter your League ID on the Setup screen and use the ready-made history export URL shown in the Historical Archive panel.
  2. Press Ctrl+S (Windows) or Cmd+S (Mac) to save the page.
  3. When asked, save it as a .json file to somewhere easy like your Desktop.
  4. Back on the Setup screen, open Load Archive JSON and drag that .json file into the dropzone (or tap it to pick the file).
The moment it loads, the Record Book and Rivalry Vault unlock. Any authenticated league member — not just the commissioner — can save the archive plus an encrypted cookie envelope to the league's Supabase record. Teammates can then load by League ID without receiving the raw credentials.
